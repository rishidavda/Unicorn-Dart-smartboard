  logo-full-dark.png   The full house lockup (wordmark + SNOOKER - POOL - BAR

Both logos came from thewinchestersc.co.uk. To use a different one, drop in a
new logo.svg or logo.png. Wide images are treated as the wordmark and shown
whole with the tagline beneath; squarish images sit beside the venue name.
Refresh the screens after changing a file.
