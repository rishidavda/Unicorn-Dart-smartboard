Smartboard

The number next to the board's rim button — if every score is wrong by the same rotation, this is why. Easier: press Line up board and throw one dart into the 20.

Throw a dart with the board connected — anything it sends shows here.

Connected but darts not counting? Look at the light on the board's rim button. Green means the board is in scoring mode; red means it is not — press Wake board and watch it change. If it is green and darts still do not register, the batteries are the usual cause: three AA cells in the back.

BUTTON on the line above means the rim button works, so the Bluetooth link is fine; DART is the one that scores.
