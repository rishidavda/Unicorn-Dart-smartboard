Board not connecting? Work down this list

Each dartboard has its own W shortcut on the desktop and its own black window. Do the steps in order and stop as soon as a dart scores on that oche's TV.

The game carries on while you fix it. The TV says BOARD NOT CONNECTED — SCORING BY TABLET: players tap their scores on the iPad's board until the dartboard is back. Nothing is lost.

Which shortcut is which board

  • Each W shortcut starts one board. Its black window's title bar names the board and its port, for example Board 2 - WinchesterDarts - port 8081.
  • Rename the two shortcuts to match (right-click, Rename), for example Board 1 - Front and Board 2 - Back.
  • Only ever restart the board that is broken. The other one keeps working.

The steps

  1. Wake the board 10 seconds

    Throw a dart at it or press its rim button, then wait 10 seconds. The PC keeps looking for the board by itself, and most drops end here.

  2. Press Fix board connection 15 seconds

    Staff console, that board's card, Board & sound, Fix board connection. Wait about 15 seconds for the message:

    • reconnected and armed: throw a dart. Done.
    • is still connecting - no need to press Fix again: wait another 10 seconds.
    • still not connected: go to step 3. If the message says to switch Bluetooth off and on, go to step 5.

  3. Free the board 30 seconds

    A board talks to one device at a time. Close the Unicorn app on any phone near the board. Then take one battery out of the board for 5 seconds, put it back and wait 10 seconds. Not back? Press Fix board connection again. Flat batteries also cause this: fit three fresh AA cells.

  4. Restart that board's window 1 minute

    Close that board's black window (check the title bar), then double-click its own W shortcut. Wait a minute. The game and the timer carry on where they were.

  5. Turn the PC's Bluetooth off and on 30 seconds

    Press Windows key + A and click the Bluetooth tile so it goes grey. Wait 5 seconds and click it again so it lights up. Both boards reconnect by themselves within a few seconds.

  6. Restart the PC 5 minutes

    When Windows is back, double-click both W shortcuts if their black windows don't open by themselves.

  7. Send the diagnostics

    On that board's card, under Board & sound, next to Diagnostics, tap open the report (a copy is saved as diagnostics.txt in that board's folder). Send it to whoever looks after the darts system.

Two cards on one dartboard. Throw a dart at this oche's board. On the card whose TV does not score it: Board & sound, Choose a different board, throw a dart at that oche's own board and tap it in the list. Then check each TV scores its own board.

Connected, but darts don't score? Look at the rim button. Red: press Wake board on the card and watch it turn green. Green but still nothing: fit fresh batteries.

Never copy one board's folder to make the other, and don't open the same W shortcut twice (it only says it is already running).
