Start the darts each day

Do this at the start of each day before you open, and any time the darts need a fresh start. It takes about five minutes and leaves everything working: the Surface, both TVs and both iPads. Each dartboard has its own W icon on the Surface's desktop.

  1. Close everything down 30 seconds

    • Close both black windows: tap the X at the top right of each one. Minimised? Tap them on the taskbar first. None open? Carry on.
    • Close any browser windows showing the darts screens.
    • Wait 10 seconds.

    This is safe: games, timers and takings are saved and come back. Timers keep counting while the windows are closed, so do it between games if you can.

  2. Open the two W icons 30 seconds

    • Double-tap one W icon on the desktop. A black window opens. Wait until it says DARTS is running:
    ==================================================== THE WINCHESTER - DARTS is running ====================================================
    • A few seconds later that board's TV screen opens full screen in the browser. That's normal: tap the – at its top right to minimise it and get back to the desktop.
    • Double-tap the other W icon. The same happens again.
    • You now have two black windows, one per board; the bar at the top of each names its board and port. Leave them open all day (minimising is fine). Closing one stops that board until you open its W again.

  3. Open the staff page and log in 1 minute

    • Open Edge from the taskbar, tap the address bar at the top, type localhost:8080/staff and press Enter.
    • On the keypad, tap the PIN 1234. It opens by itself after the fourth number. It asks again whenever the page has been left for a while: just tap 1234 again.
    • There is a card for each board (scroll down for the second one). If a card says Powered off, tap its POWER ON.
    • Throw a dart at each board to wake it. Within a minute the tag at the top right of each card should say BOARD READY.

  4. Refresh the TVs 1 minute

    • On each TV, reload the darts page in its browser (the reload button, or F5 on a keyboard).
    • Then click once on the page (or press OK on its remote). Until something touches the page after a reload, the caller's voice stays silent. To check the sound: on that board's staff card open BOARD & SOUND and tap TEST CALLER ON TV; the TV calls "One hundred and eighty!".
    • Check each TV shows its own board's name in gold at the top right. Throw a dart at that board: the TV should say ● BOARD IS LIVE — … DETECTED.

  5. Restart the iPad apps 1 minute each

    On each players' iPad, one per board:

    • Turn Guided Access off: press the top button (or the Home button, on iPads that have one) three times quickly; don't hold it down. Type the Guided Access passcode (the manager has it). If a list appears, tap Guided Access. Then tap End at the top left.
    • Close the darts app: slowly swipe up from the bottom edge and stop in the middle of the screen until the open apps appear as cards (or double-click the Home button). Swipe the darts app's card up off the top of the screen.
    • Open it again: on the Home Screen, tap the darts app's icon (usually named The Winchester…), then tap once on the venue name at the top left of the darts screen (it keeps the screen awake). The top right should say BOARD READY and CONNECTED.
    • Turn Guided Access back on: press the top (or Home) button three times quickly. If a list appears, tap Guided Access. If the Guided Access screen opens, tap Start at the top right. If it just says Guided Access Started, it is on: don't press the button again. Always leave the iPads like this.

  6. Start a session when customers pay

    • On that board's card, type the member's name in Member name — who's paying?
    • Tap 1 HOUR or 2 HOURS. The card says armed — waiting for their first game: their time starts with their first game on the iPad (straight away if a game is already going).
    • Another length: type the minutes in Custom minutes and tap START.
    • Paying at the end: tap STOPWATCH (PAY AT END). When they finish, tap END NOW and the card shows what to charge.
    • More time: +15 MIN or +30 MIN. END NOW stops a session early.
