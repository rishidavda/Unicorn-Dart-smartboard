# Staff training video — narration script

`winchester-staff-guide.mp4` · 4 min 28 s · 14 scenes.
Regenerate: screenshots via `shots.js`/`cards.js`, narration via Piper (en_GB-alan),
assembly via `build.js` (scene image + voice, concatenated).

| # | Scene | Narration |
|---|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Title card | Welcome to the Winchester darts system. This is the staff guide: the staff console, selling time, the leaderboard, and fixing the usual problems in under a minute. |
| 2 | Diagram card | Each oche is one Windows PC — its own board, TV and players' iPad. One staff iPad runs every board from a single page, and the leaderboard merges them all. Remember the split when something misbehaves: board trouble is Bluetooth or batteries; screen trouble is Wi-Fi or the browser. |
| 3 | Connect page | Turn a PC on and Winchester Darts starts with it. Its screen page shows the address for every view — point a camera at the QR codes. On each TV, tap the page once: that single tap unlocks the announcer for the day. |
| 4 | Staff PIN gate | The staff console lives at slash staff, behind a PIN — one, two, three, four until you change it. One PIN runs every board, and the console locks itself thirty seconds after you walk away. |
| 5 | Staff console (all boards) | One card per board. Take payment, then one hour, two hours, or any number of minutes. Customers cannot start a game until you've put time on the clock, and the countdown starts with their first game — not when you press the button. For pay-at-the-end, press Stopwatch instead: the clock counts up and never expires — press End now when they settle up. Add fifteen or thirty minutes mid-session, or end a session early. At zero the oche closes itself: the game ends and the names clear. |
| 6 | Players iPad, closed | Between sessions the players' iPad shows this. It unlocks the moment you start a timer, and closes again at zero — the next group inherits nothing. |
| 7 | Board & sound fold-out | Under Board and sound on each card: Connect, Wake board — watch the board's rim light turn green — Line up board if every score is wrong by the same rotation, the caller test, and the diagnostics report. And watch the battery pill: a flat board keeps its button working long after darts stop counting. Three double-A cells in the back. |
| 8 | Venue leaderboard | At slash board, the whole venue's leaderboard: the top fifty for the month, the all-time top ten, and the record books — best visit, most one-eighties in a game, quickest win — with the latest results from every board. Read-only, safe on any screen. |
| 9 | Game picker (step 2 of the wizard) | Customers run their own games in two steps: add player names — remembered for the session — then pick from twenty-four games in four groups: classics like five-oh-one and Cricket, party games like Killer, Gotcha and Sudden Death, score races, and practice drills like Bob's twenty-seven. |
| 10 | How to play sheet | Nobody has to ask the bar how a game works: every card has a How to play button with the rules in plain English. Mid-session, the Fix tab restarts the game or takes the same players back to the menu to pick another. |
| 11 | Play screen | During a game, everything fits one screen. Scores at the top, next player, undo dart and miss below, and a picture of the board to tap for bounce-outs — or to play without the smartboard entirely. |
| 12 | TV with 180 | The TV shows every dart as it lands. When a turn ends, it holds the player's three darts and their total for ten seconds, and the announcer calls it out loud — one hundred and eighty included. |
| 13 | Fix-it card | Four fixes solve almost everything. Beeps but no voice: tap that TV's page once. The button works but darts don't count: wake the board from the staff console, then fresh batteries. Scores rotated round the board: line up board, one dart in the twenty. Anything else: the diagnostics report on the board's card — there's always a copy in diagnostics dot text, next to that PC's exe. |
| 14 | Outro card | That's the lot. PIN one two three four. Diagnostics dot text if support asks. And for board hardware, Unicorn are on 0115, 985, 3500. Game on! |
