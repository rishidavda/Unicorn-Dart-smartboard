# Staff training video — narration script

`winchester-staff-guide.mp4` · 3 min 26 s · 11 scenes.
Regenerate: screenshots via `shots.js`/`cards.js`, narration via Piper (en_GB-alan),
assembly via `build.js` (scene image + voice, concatenated).

| # | Scene | Narration |
|---|-------|-----------|
| 1 | Title card | Welcome to the Winchester darts system. This is the staff guide: starting up, the settings PIN, the customer timer, and fixing the usual problems in under a minute. |
| 2 | Diagram card | One Windows PC runs everything. The dartboard talks to it over Bluetooth, and the TV and iPad are just web browsers. Remember that split: if the board misbehaves, it's Bluetooth or batteries. If a screen freezes, it's Wi-Fi or the browser. |
| 3 | Connect page | Turn the PC on and Winchester Darts starts with it. This page shows the address for each screen — point the iPad's camera at the QR code. And on the TV, tap the page once: that single tap unlocks all sound for the day. |
| 4 | PIN keypad | Settings are staff only, behind a PIN. It's one, two, three, four, until you change it. Once you're in, the tab stays unlocked on that iPad until you lock it again or close the browser. |
| 5 | Board controls | The board controls live here. Connect board finds the board automatically. The light on the board's rim button tells the truth: green means scoring, red means asleep — Wake board turns it green. If every score is wrong by the same amount, press Line up board and throw one dart into the twenty. And keep an eye on the battery line: a flat board keeps its button working long after darts stop counting. Three double-A cells in the back. |
| 6 | Customer timer | Selling time takes two taps. One hour, two hours, or any number of minutes. The clock starts when their first game starts — not when you press the button — and shows on both screens. When it hits zero, the game they're in finishes, but no new game can start until you set a new timer. |
| 7 | Game setup | Customers run their own games: seven to choose from — five-oh-one, Cricket, Around the Clock, Count-up, Killer, Shanghai and Halve It. Type a player's name once and it's remembered. |
| 8 | Play screen | During a game, everything fits one screen. Scores at the top, next player, undo dart and miss below, and a picture of the board to tap for bounce-outs — or to play without the smartboard entirely. |
| 9 | TV with 180 | The TV shows every dart as it lands. When a turn ends, it holds the player's three darts and their total for ten seconds, and the announcer calls it out loud — one hundred and eighty included. |
| 10 | Fix-it card | Four fixes solve almost everything. Beeps but no voice: tap the TV page once. The button works but darts don't count: wake the board, then fresh batteries. Scores rotated round the board: line up board, one dart in the twenty. Anything else: board diagnostics, copy for support — and there's always a copy in diagnostics dot text, next to the exe. |
| 11 | Outro card | That's the lot. PIN one two three four. Diagnostics dot text if support asks. And for board hardware, Unicorn are on 0115, 985, 3500. Game on! |
